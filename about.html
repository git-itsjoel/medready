About MedReady

MedReady is a centralized platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and pharmacy inventory. We understand the frustration of visiting multiple hospitals just to find a specific medicine.

Our Mission

To provide real-time visibility into medicine availability across city hospitals, ensuring patients can locate essential medications instantly and avoid unnecessary travel during emergencies.

How It Works

  • 🏥 For Hospitals: Staff can easily update stock status (Available/Out of Stock).
  • 🔍 For Patients: Search by hospital, medicine name, or category to find what you need.
  • ⚡ Real-Time: Updates made by staff are instantly reflected for searchers.
